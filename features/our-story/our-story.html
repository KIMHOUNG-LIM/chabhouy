Why We Started ChabHouy

Phnom Penh & Battambang • Founded 2022
"Every grain of Jasmine rice from Battambang and every pod of Kampot Pepper carries the dedication of a Cambodian farming family. Our goal is to ensure they receive fair income while delivering healthy food to every home."

Before ChabHouy, local farmers faced price volatility and middleman exploitation. We built a direct farm-to-door ecosystem that guarantees fair wages for growers and pesticide-free produce for families.

Our Founding Vision

Preserving Agricultural Heritage, Empowering Local Communities

Cambodia boasts some of the world's finest natural agricultural treasures — award-winning Phka Rumduol Jasmine Rice, Geographical Indication (GI) certified Kampot Pepper, and traditional Kampong Thom Palm Sugar.

Direct Farmer Partnership

We work directly with farming cooperatives in Battambang, Kampot, and Kampong Thom without intermediaries.

Strict Quality & Organic Guarantee

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks to guarantee 100% natural, pesticide-free freshness.

Sustainable Eco-Packaging

We use biodegradable leaf wrappings and recyclable containers to protect Cambodia's natural environment.
